# Content Systems

**Status:** Open

This document tracks the major kinds of repeatable and authored content Rookvale may use. Nothing here is automatically committed merely because it is listed.

## Candidate content families

- overworld enemy encounters;
- elite enemies;
- field bosses;
- dungeons;
- party dungeons;
- raids / large cooperative bosses;
- quests and NPC stories;
- hidden encounters;
- puzzles / environmental challenges;
- gathering;
- crafting;
- treasure / exploration rewards;
- world events;
- bounty or hunt systems;
- class-specific challenges;
- collection systems;
- towns and social spaces;
- seasonal or rotating activities.

## Content design principle

Each system should answer at least one of these questions:

- What new decision does this create?
- What new place does this make worth visiting?
- What new reason does this create to cooperate?
- What new build or progression choice does this support?
- What new story/world information does it expose?

If it answers none of them, it is likely content bloat.

## Open questions

The final content mix will be decided after the core combat, exploration, and progression loops are clearer.
