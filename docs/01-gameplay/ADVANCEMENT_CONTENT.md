# Advancement Content

**Status:** Core advancement-content philosophy locked; exact quests, encounter scripts, Tier names, and difficulty numbers remain Draft

## Purpose

Major advancement in Rookvale should feel like an event in the character's journey rather than a menu button or a repeated generic exam.

This applies to both:

- **Character Tier promotion** — broad character/world progression;
- **Class advancement / promotion** — advancement within a class lineage.

The content used to advance may be called an **Advancement Quest**, **Promotion Journey**, class quest, rite, trial, incident, investigation, expedition, or another setting-appropriate name. `Trial` is only one possible form and is not the universal structure.

## Mixed-content rule

Advancement content does **not** have to be combat-only.

A promotion sequence may contain any meaningful combination of:

- conversations and NPC requests;
- investigation;
- exploration or navigation;
- discovering a location or route;
- environmental interaction;
- puzzle or observation tasks;
- escort, protection, rescue, or survival events;
- choices or preparation steps;
- class-specific mechanic demonstrations;
- ordinary combat;
- Elite or Boss combat;
- multi-stage encounters;
- short dungeons;
- longer questlines or world incidents.

Some promotions may be short. Important promotions may be substantially longer if the content is varied and narratively justified.

Do not force every Tier and every class to use the same template, length, boss count, or objective count.

## Character Tier promotion

The current baseline launch structure is:

```text
Tier I      Lv. 1-10
Tier II     Lv.11-20
Tier III    Lv.21-30
Tier IV     Lv.31-40
Tier V      Lv.41-50
Tier VI     Lv.51-60
Tier VII    Lv.61-70
Tier VIII   Lv.71-80
```

This is the **initial progression plan**, not a promise that Rookvale can never expand beyond Lv.80 / Tier VIII.

Future expansions may add higher Character Levels, higher Tiers, or other progression layers when the game has enough new content to justify them. Do not reserve empty Tiers merely for hypothetical future expansion, and do not design current numbers around infinite vertical inflation.

A normal Tier promotion requires a combination of:

1. reaching the current Tier's level cap;
2. completing sufficient personal progression / milestone requirements;
3. completing that Tier's Advancement Quest or Promotion Journey.

The exact milestone may vary by Tier and may allow more than one qualifying route when open-world freedom benefits from it.

Tier promotion may grant:

- access to the next Character Level band;
- access to the next reward band;
- access to higher-tier equipment and materials;
- new regions or progression content where appropriate;
- a noticeable but controlled immediate baseline power increase;
- new systems where appropriate.

## Class advancement

Class advancement follows the same content philosophy but tests the identity of the lineage rather than generic account progression.

Requirements may combine:

- Character Level or Tier;
- prerequisite class unlocks;
- prerequisite Class Mastery;
- class-related NPC questlines;
- discovery of a teacher, faction, location, artifact, event, or technique;
- class-specific Advancement Quest content.

Different lineages may advance in radically different ways.

Examples of structure, not locked content:

- a Knight promotion may involve protecting people through a crisis and surviving a final defense;
- a Duelist promotion may revolve around investigation and a formal duel;
- a Hunter promotion may be a tracking expedition ending in a dangerous hunt;
- an Oracle promotion may require interpreting clues, predicting encounter behavior, and resolving a final event;
- an Artificer promotion might involve obtaining components, repairing a device, and then using it in an encounter.

Do not reduce class advancement to repetitive counters such as `Block 30 attacks`, `cast Heal 50 times`, or `apply Burn 100 times`.

## Skill requirement without permanent lockout

Major progression content should require some degree of player understanding and execution. Promotion should not be completely automatic.

However, mandatory advancement must not become an indefinite skill wall that causes an otherwise progressing player to be permanently unable to unlock the next Tier or class.

### Normal character growth must matter

Advancement content normally uses the player's **actual character** rather than a fully normalized fixed-stat avatar.

Therefore:

- Character Level matters;
- Character Tier baseline matters;
- appropriate equipment matters;
- class build and loadout matter;
- consumables and preparation may matter where allowed;
- Class Mastery options may matter;
- better gear or a better-prepared build can make the same promotion easier.

This means a player who struggles mechanically can return after improving equipment, changing build, learning more class options, or preparing better and experience a materially easier attempt.

Do not make ordinary mandatory promotions ignore progression by hard-fixing every player's stats to an identical challenge loadout.

Normalized challenge modes may exist elsewhere as optional prestige content, but they are not the default model for required progression.

### Failure assistance

If a mandatory solo/instanced advancement segment proves to be a repeated blocker, Rookvale may offer escalating assistance after failures rather than permanently lowering the entire game's difficulty.

Possible assistance tools include:

- clearer telegraph explanations or hints;
- an optional temporary blessing for that attempt;
- increased HP / damage / healing for the instance;
- reduced enemy damage or mechanic intensity;
- stronger allied NPC support;
- extra recovery resources;
- a recommended-build / equipment warning;
- an alternate objective route where the fiction supports it.

Assistance should be explicit and optional where practical. A player who wants the original challenge can keep attempting the normal version.

Completing a mandatory promotion with assistance still counts as legitimate progression. Do not create a second-class Tier or permanently mark the character as weaker because the player used accessibility/progression assistance.

## Party and carry interaction

Advancement content may use solo, NPC-assisted, party, or mixed stages depending on the event.

However:

- Character Tier advancement remains **personal**;
- every character must own and progress their own advancement state;
- another player's completed quest cannot directly grant the promotion;
- a party member cannot simply open a later Tier for someone who has not met that character's prerequisites;
- party-help stages may exist, but important personal steps remain attributable to the advancing character.

Rookvale does not need to make every promotion strictly solo merely to stop carrying. The existing personal Tier, milestone, and reward-eligibility rules prevent one carry kill from skipping the whole progression ladder.

## Difficulty target

Mandatory promotion content should generally test whether the player understands the important mechanics introduced so far, not whether they can execute near-perfect endgame play.

Target behavior:

- an appropriately leveled and reasonably equipped player with average execution can clear it;
- a skilled player can clear earlier/cleaner;
- a struggling player can improve character power, preparation, or use available assistance;
- exceptional optional versions may exist for prestige rewards, but are not required for basic progression.

## Variety across Tiers

Tier promotion content should evolve with the game rather than repeat `enter arena -> kill boss -> promote` eight times.

Possible progression of emphasis:

- early promotions teach core combat/readability;
- middle promotions combine exploration, world systems, status/formation/Break understanding, and encounters;
- later promotions may become longer multi-stage incidents, expeditions, dungeon-like sequences, or major world events;
- final launch-era promotion can be a meaningful culmination of the initial progression arc.

Exact content is designed alongside the world, quests, and Tier-specific systems.

## Explicitly rejected

- one identical arena exam for every Tier and class;
- combat-only advancement as a universal rule;
- fixed-stat mandatory trials that make normal character growth irrelevant;
- mandatory progression tuned like optional hardcore content;
- permanent progression lockout because a player has slightly weaker execution;
- skill-use counters as the main class-promotion requirement;
- another player's quest completion automatically granting personal Tier/class advancement;
- hiding important advancement requirements until after a player has already invested substantial effort.
