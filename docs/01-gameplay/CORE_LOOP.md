# Core Gameplay Loop

**Status:** Draft

## Macro loop

1. Prepare in a town, hub, or safe location.
2. Enter the open world and choose a direction or objective.
3. Explore locations, encounter enemies, discover events, interact with NPCs, and find content.
4. Enter turn-based battles when appropriate.
5. Gain progression resources, equipment, knowledge, access, or other rewards.
6. Make build, class, equipment, party, or route decisions.
7. Unlock or survive increasingly demanding areas and activities.
8. Return to social/safe spaces as useful, then venture out again.

## Multiplayer loop

The same loop should work solo, but parties should add tactical possibilities rather than simply multiplying damage. A healer, tank, controller, buffer, burst attacker, or hybrid should alter how the group approaches encounters.

## Desired exploration behavior

- The world should offer visible and hidden reasons to deviate from the shortest route.
- Regions should have identity beyond enemy level differences.
- Discovery should include more than treasure chests.
- Players should sometimes encounter content before they are ready for it.
- The starting region should teach systems naturally through exploration rather than functioning as a long tutorial corridor.

## Open design questions

- Encounter initiation: contact, interaction, proximity, aggression radius, or mixed?
- Are enemies persistent on the map, dynamically spawned, or both?
- How strongly does death punish exploration?
- How much can players fast travel?
- What non-combat activities are core rather than side distractions?
