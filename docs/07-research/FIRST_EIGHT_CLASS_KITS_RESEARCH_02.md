# First Eight Class Kits — Broad Research Pass 02

**Status:** Active design research

This pass covers the seven first-slice advanced classes after Knight: Duelist, Hunter, Marksman, Pyromancer, Cryomancer, Priest, and Oracle. The goal is not to reproduce one external class kit. Each Rookvale kit synthesizes mechanics from multiple RPGs, then adapts them to Rookvale's sequential Speed timeline, one-natural-action rule, front/back rows, threat, class resources, Reaction slot, Signature slot, and solo/multiplayer requirements.

## Research pool sampled

The pass deliberately mixed famous and less-obvious references.

### Counter / Duelist references
- Darkest Dungeon / Darkest Dungeon II: Highwayman and Duelist mobility, Riposte, positional attack patterns, setup before retaliation.
- Guild Wars 2 Spellbreaker: Full Counter, boon removal, trading raw burst for precision/control.
- Fell Seal: dedicated Counter layer and explicit prevention of counter-to-counter recursion.

### Hunter / companion references
- Guild Wars 2 Ranger: permanent animal companion as profession mechanic with player-commanded pet abilities.
- World of Warcraft Hunter: Focus resource, pet-heavy Beast Mastery versus precision-heavy Marksmanship, traps and survival tools.
- Last Epoch Falconer: persistent companion plus commands, trap setup, net/mark interactions, and companion attacks that are empowered by player setup.
- Darkest Dungeon Houndmaster / mark systems: target marking, protection reduction, guard/support and coordinated prey pressure.

### Marksman references
- World of Warcraft Marksmanship: deliberate high-value shots, Focus management, long-range identity and standing/setup incentives.
- Darkest Dungeon Arbalest/Musketeer design: mark -> amplified precision shot, suppression/debuff utility, limited healing/support.
- Darkest Dungeon Highwayman: ranged target coverage and preparation skills that improve later attacks.
- Tactical RPG gunner/sniper archetypes: commitment and target-reading over generic rapid-fire spam.

### Pyromancer references
- Divinity: Original Sin 2 Pyrokinetic: Burning plus haste/support, direct fire, ignition and consuming existing Burning for stronger effects.
- Last Epoch / ARPG fire archetypes: ignite stacking, conversion, skill-specific specialization and choosing between direct burst and damage-over-time.
- Darkest Dungeon II Runaway: Burn as a tactical status rather than only a damage color; interaction with existing fire states and support/utility.
- Siralim-style specialization lessons: a class mechanic should make an entire kit behave differently rather than merely add fire damage.

### Cryomancer references
- Turn-based freeze/chill archetypes across tactical RPGs: separate soft control (Slow/Chill) from hard control (Freeze), with boss-safe control conversion.
- Elemental RPG design such as Bravely / Divinity / class-heavy MMOs: defensive ice, barriers and control can distinguish cold magic from fire's attrition.
- Rookvale's own boss-break rule: hard Freeze against bosses should normally contribute to Break/Frostbite states rather than repeatedly deleting boss turns.

### Priest references
- Darkest Dungeon Vestal: reliable direct healing versus proactive prevention/control; cautionary lesson that a pure healer with little utility becomes one-note.
- Bravely Default II Spiritmaster: regeneration, scheduled recovery, cleanse spirits, Reraise and persistent support effects.
- Siralim Ultimate Cleric: healing that creates barriers/buffs and turns recovery into broader defensive value.
- FFXIV/GW2 healer-support patterns: healing, barriers, cleanse, rescue and damage-support should coexist without requiring constant HP spam.

### Oracle references
- Final Fantasy XIV Astrologian: card/arcana support, delayed Horoscope-style payoff, healing/support tied to prediction/fate fantasy.
- Bravely Default II Oracle: Haste/Slow and elemental manipulation as fate/time support rather than direct nuking.
- Guild Wars 2 Chronomancer: local timeline manipulation and carefully constrained rewind-style concepts; strong warning against unrestricted cooldown/turn loops.
- Tactical games with intent telegraphs: information itself can be a combat resource if it changes player decisions.

## Cross-kit rules derived from the pass

1. **No class should require enemy kills for its basic loop.** Kill triggers may be bonuses only.
2. **Same origin, different play loop.** Hunter and Marksman both come from Ranger but one coordinates prey/companion/traps while the other converts preparation into precise shots.
3. **Soft control before hard control.** Chill, aim disruption, marks, threat manipulation and timeline delay remain useful when hard CC is resisted.
4. **Reaction is not a second turn.** Counter, emergency heal and predictive intervention follow the already-locked Reaction rules.
5. **Healing should create decisions.** Priest must choose between direct repair, prevention, cleanse, delayed protection and rescue rather than press the largest heal every turn.
6. **Oracle may manipulate future information and action timing, but cannot create infinite turn/cooldown loops.**
7. **Elemental classes must differ mechanically.** Pyromancer is attrition/combustion/resource conversion; Cryomancer is control/protection/shatter timing.
8. **Companions must remain commandable and readable.** Hunter's beast should not become an uncontrolled extra character that doubles turn count.
9. **Precision classes need payoff for setup without becoming unusable when setup fails.** Marksman keeps baseline attacks and can cash out Aim/Mark for burst.
10. **Solo function is mandatory.** Party utility is additive, not the only reason a class works.

## Production boundary

All seven kits created from this research pass remain Draft. Names, exact cooldowns, resource caps and numerical coefficients are prototype values. Their mechanical identities and internal contrasts should be tested before being locked.
