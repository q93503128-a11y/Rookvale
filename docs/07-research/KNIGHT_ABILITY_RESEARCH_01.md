# Knight Ability Research 01

**Status:** Research input for draft kit; not a locked class implementation

This pass studies defensive/frontline classes from both large and smaller games. The goal is not to copy a complete kit. The useful patterns are extracted and rebuilt around Rookvale's sequential Speed timeline, one-standard-action turns, front/back formation, weighted threat, turn cooldowns, reactions, and class-specific resources.

## References reviewed

### Darkest Dungeon / Darkest Dungeon II — Man-at-Arms
Sources:
- https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Man-at-Arms_(Darkest_Dungeon_II)
- https://darkestdungeon.wiki.gg/wiki/Man-at-Arms/Strategy

Useful ideas:
- Tanking is a combination of **Taunt, Guard, Block, movement/repositioning, party support, and Riposte**, not merely high Defense.
- Guard has insurance value even on turns when it is not triggered because allies can play more aggressively around it.
- A tank can remain tactically useful while not taking damage through buffs, debuffs, repositioning, and retaliation setup.
- Distinct paths can reinterpret one defensive class as command-support, pure bulwark, or more aggressive counter-tank.

Rookvale adaptation:
- Knight should be able to protect, redirect, control target pressure, reposition rows, and turn successful defense into future tempo.
- Avoid making every Knight turn a self-defense button.

### Final Fantasy XIV — Paladin
Source:
- https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/jobguide/paladin

Useful ideas:
- A dedicated defensive resource can pay for both self-protection and ally-protection tools.
- Cover / Intervention / Sheltron separate three different decisions: take danger yourself, reduce danger to an ally, or harden yourself.
- Resource identity works best when it is tied to normal class behavior rather than a detached ultimate meter.

Rookvale adaptation:
- Knight's Resolve should power several kinds of defensive decisions instead of one mandatory spender.
- Rookvale must not reproduce MMO real-time rotation structure or exact skill behavior.

### Tactics Ogre — Guardian Force
Reference:
- https://www.neoseeker.com/tactics-ogre-wheel-of-fate/faqs/237347-tactics-ogre-psp-class.html

Useful idea:
- Redirecting damage from nearby allies creates a very clear protector identity, but unlimited redirection can kill the tank too quickly.

Rookvale adaptation:
- Interception requires caps, mitigation, Resolve costs, or event limits so one tank cannot accidentally absorb an entire party wipe in a single enemy sequence.

### Wartales — Protector / Swordsman
References:
- https://wartales.fandom.com/wiki/Wartales_Best_Builds_(Opinion)
- https://progameguides.com/wartales/best-wartales-swordsman-build/

Useful ideas:
- Defensive value can be generated by **engaging** the dangerous enemy rather than passively existing.
- Deflection/Protection rewards the tank for taking ownership of a threat.
- A tank resource loop tied to actual defensive behavior feels more purposeful than passive per-turn regeneration.

Rookvale adaptation:
- Resolve generation should come from being targeted, defending, or intercepting, not automatic `+1 every natural turn`.

### Siralim Ultimate — Paladin / Siegemaster
References:
- https://siralimultimate.wiki.gg/wiki/Specializations
- https://siralimultimate.wiki.gg/wiki/Siegemaster

Useful ideas:
- Provoke and Defend can be made into offensive engines through retaliation, thorns, cleansing, stat conversion, and trigger-based passives.
- Strong passives can change what a basic combat command means.

Rookvale adaptation:
- Knight's Defend command should be allowed to interact with class passives and Resolve.
- Retaliation should be a build path, not mandatory baseline damage.

### Battle Brothers — tank perk interactions
References:
- https://steamah.com/battle-brothers-in-depth-perks-guide-2020/
- https://gamerofpassion.com/battle-brothers-perk-guide/

Useful ideas and warnings:
- Taunt is tactically useful but competes with self-protection if both consume the same action.
- Rotation / ally swapping is a strong tank utility because it rescues fragile allies without merely adding damage reduction.
- Indomitable-style protection plus initiative manipulation can create degenerate near-permanent defense loops.
- Pure tanks risk feeling like dead weight when the encounter does not currently require protection.

Rookvale adaptation:
- Knight needs proactive attacks, break contribution, row manipulation, and retaliation options in addition to pure protection.
- Defensive durations should avoid loopholes where low/high Speed can create unintended permanent mitigation.

## Synthesis for Rookvale Knight

The Knight should not be defined as `highest Defense`. Its identity is:

1. **Own the dangerous target** through weighted threat / Challenge.
2. **Move danger away from allies** through Guard, Intercept, and row rescue.
3. **Survive chosen danger** through Block / Bulwark rather than passive invulnerability.
4. **Earn Resolve by doing tank work**, including single-boss encounters.
5. **Convert good defense into tempo** through retaliation, break, or stronger follow-up actions.
6. **Remain useful when protection is not urgently needed** through pressure, break, repositioning, and support.

## Resolve design principles

Resolve must function in solo, low-enemy-count, and boss battles.

Prototype direction:
- no automatic universal `+1 Resolve per natural turn`;
- gain Resolve from being targeted by a hostile natural action;
- gain additional Resolve when damage is successfully intercepted / redirected from an ally;
- Defend may generate Resolve because it is an intentional defensive action;
- per-hostile-action generation caps prevent multi-hit attacks from instantly filling the bar;
- Resolve is spent on high-value defense, interception, retaliation setup, or control;
- a modest cap prevents indefinite hoarding.

This avoids giving Speed a third automatic scaling axis on top of extra natural turns and faster natural-turn cooldown cycling.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:
- passive `Defense +X%` as the entire class mechanic;
- universal back-row untargetability while Knight is alive;
- permanent taunt that overwrites boss mechanics;
- intercepting every attack for free;
- a tank who has nothing useful to do when enemies already target them;
- Resolve that only appears when multiple enemies die or many adds spawn;
- defensive durations that can be extended indefinitely through Speed/timeline abuse;
- mandatory shield-only identity so strong that ancestral weapon variety becomes fake.
