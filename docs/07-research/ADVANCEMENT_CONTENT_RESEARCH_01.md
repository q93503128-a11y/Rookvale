# Advancement Content Research 01

**Date:** 2026-10-16

## Question

How should Rookvale gate major Character Tier promotions and class advancement without reducing them to menu clicks, generic combat exams, or permanent mechanical-skill walls?

## Useful external patterns

### Final Fantasy XIV — quest instance difficulty assistance

FFXIV allows players who fail many quest instance battles to retry at lower difficulty settings. Easy and Very Easy grant large temporary combat advantages and may alter encounter parameters. The important lesson is not to copy those exact percentages, but that **mandatory narrative/progression content can preserve a normal challenge while offering explicit fallback assistance after failure**.

Implication for Rookvale:

- advancement content may test mechanics;
- repeated failure should not permanently stop ordinary progression;
- assistance can be limited to the mandatory instance rather than globally reducing difficulty;
- using assistance should not create a permanently inferior character.

Official reference:
- FINAL FANTASY XIV Patch 5.1 Notes, quest instance battle difficulty settings.

### Final Fantasy XIV — level / item requirements and personal unlocks

FFXIV Duties commonly expose required character level and, in later content, required item level and prerequisite completion. This demonstrates the value of showing progression requirements explicitly before entry.

Implication for Rookvale:

- Tier/class advancement prerequisites should be visible;
- the game should warn about missing requirements before the player invests time;
- actual character growth and equipment can remain relevant to required content.

Official references:
- FINAL FANTASY XIV Duty database / game manual.

### WARFRAME — Mastery rank tests

Warframe uses explicit tests to promote Mastery Rank, reinforcing the feeling that a rank is an earned milestone rather than only accumulated XP.

Useful lesson:

- a large visible progression rank benefits from a ceremonial or gameplay confirmation event.

Rejected part for Rookvale:

- do not make every promotion the same short exam template;
- Rookvale's handcrafted world should allow quests, incidents, expeditions, exploration, NPC stories, puzzles, combat, and mixed sequences.

Official reference:
- WARFRAME Support, Mastery Basics.

## Rookvale synthesis

Rookvale should treat promotion as **advancement content**, not universally as a `Trial`.

The form may vary by class and Tier:

- NPC questline;
- world incident;
- investigation;
- expedition;
- exploration objective;
- puzzle/observation sequence;
- survival/rescue/protection event;
- combat or boss;
- short dungeon;
- multi-stage mixed quest.

Major mandatory advancement should require reasonable understanding but not hardcore execution. Normal Character Level, Tier baseline, equipment, class build, Class Mastery choices, preparation, and permitted consumables should influence difficulty. Fully normalized fixed-stat challenges are better reserved for optional prestige content.

When repeated failure threatens to become a progression wall, explicit instance-specific assistance is preferable to either permanent lockout or globally nerfing the game.

## Locked design conclusions

- Initial launch-era progression target: Lv.1-80, Tier I-VIII, 10 Character Levels per Tier.
- Lv.80/Tier VIII is an initial cap and may expand in a future content era.
- Tier promotion normally requires current-band level cap + personal progression milestone + advancement content.
- Class advancement may use its own distinct NPCs, discoveries, incidents, teachers, factions, quests, and mixed gameplay.
- Advancement is not combat-only.
- Advancement length may vary; important promotions may be longer when content variety justifies it.
- Actual character growth should normally make mandatory advancement easier.
- Mandatory advancement should not be tuned as prestige/endgame challenge content.
- Optional failure assistance may be offered without devaluing the resulting Tier/class advancement.
- Personal advancement state remains personal even when some stages allow party help.

## References

- FINAL FANTASY XIV, Patch 5.1 Notes — quest instance difficulty selection after defeat.
- FINAL FANTASY XIV, Game Manual / Duty database — explicit unlock, level, and item-level requirements.
- WARFRAME Support, Mastery Basics — Mastery Rank progression and rank tests.
